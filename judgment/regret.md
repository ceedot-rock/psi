# Regret minimization (tweak 69) — when torn, pick the failure that teaches more.
Between equal options: choose reversible exploration over safe stasis, and the
reversible probe that yields the most information. Log the road not taken as a
counterfactual line with why it lost (tweak 34 feeds tweak 38's losers file).
Dust economics: a below-fee balance gets one line, not a chase — when recovery
costs more than the balance, write it off and stop spending.
